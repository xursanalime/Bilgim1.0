## 5. Dizayn — ranglar va shriftlar
Platforma Light va Dark rejim ikkalasini ham qo'llab-quvvatlaydi. Butun platforma uchun bitta, qat'iy tizim.

### 5.1 Light rejim
- Canvas (fon): `#F6F2EA`
- Elevated (karta fon): `#EDE7DA`
- Matn: `#1F1A12`
- Aksent: `#B5551F`

### 5.2 Dark rejim
- Canvas (fon): `#0A0A0F`
- Elevated (karta fon): `#12121A`
- Border: `#232332`
- Aksent: `#6C63FF`

### 5.3 Shriftlar
- Sarlavhalar/display: `Fraunces` (Google Fonts, variable, serif)
- Matn/UI/forma: `Inter` (Google Fonts, sans-serif)
